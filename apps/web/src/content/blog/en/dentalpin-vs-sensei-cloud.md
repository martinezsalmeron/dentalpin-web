Sensei Cloud is Carestream Dental's cloud practice management system, and the successor to four on-premise products the same company still supports. If you are running WinOMS, SoftDent, OrthoTrac or PracticeWorks today, this is the product you are being pointed at.

We make Dentalpin, so we are not neutral. What we can be is accurate.

> **How this comparison is sourced.** Every claim about Sensei Cloud below comes from a page Carestream Dental publishes on gosensei.com, linked and dated at the end. No aggregator blogs and no review sites: they contradict each other and some are written by competitors. There is a whole section on when Sensei Cloud is the better choice, because for a lot of practices it is.

## In thirty seconds

**Sensei Cloud** is the cloud product of a company that says it has been building practice management software for more than thirty years. Imaging, electronic claims, eligibility verification, ePrescribing, payments and analytics are all in the same catalogue, training and setup are in every package, and there is a phone number with published hours on the other end.

**Dentalpin** is open source and costs nothing to license: no fee per chair, per dentist or per patient, the code published, the records on hardware you choose. In exchange it is from 2026, somebody has to own the server, and it has nothing for US insurance and no imaging.

The question that decides between them is not features. It is whether you are comfortable with what their published contract says about the day you leave.

## What Sensei Cloud is

Cloud practice management software published by Carestream Dental LLC, a Georgia limited liability company based in Atlanta. It sells into general dentistry, orthodontics, oral surgery, and enterprise or DSO groups, with a package line-up for each.

Their About page dates the work two ways in two paragraphs: "For over 35 years, our team has been at the forefront of practice management solutions", and Sensei is "the product of more than 30 years of building, refining, and reinventing practice management software". The same page names the products it grew out of, and Carestream Dental still runs a support hub for all four: WinOMS, SoftDent, OrthoTrac and PracticeWorks.

The FAQ is unambiguous about the architecture. Sensei Cloud "is fully cloud-based, meaning there is no on-site server required", and it "is designed to replace traditional server-based practice management systems, eliminating the need for in-office servers, manual updates, and complex IT maintenance".

Around the core there is a named suite of integrated tools:

- **Patient engagement.** Patient Manager for reminders, two-way texting and campaigns, plus Advanced Forms for digital intake.
- **Patient care.** Imaging for x-rays, intraoral photos and 3D images stored in the chart, and Rx Manager for prescriptions.
- **Business health.** Payment Manager, Insights Manager for analytics, eClaims for electronic claims and eVerifications for eligibility.

Periodontal charting is named on their own imaging page, in a sentence about viewing images "alongside clinical notes, periodontal charts and treatment history". That is the only mention of it on the pages consulted, so treat it as present rather than as a documented module.

### The packages, and what each one gates

There are three packages per specialty: **Yellow Belt**, **Black Belt** and **Dojo**. The base package is genuinely broad, and includes training, "Software setup, configuration, and implementation", "Unlimited users", "Unlimited cloud storage", "Multiple locations", "Data backup of Sensei Cloud", software support and updates, Payment Manager, and iTero and Quickbooks integration.

What matters for the price you end up paying is what sits above the base:

1. **The first provider only is included.** "Additional Providers" is listed under Add-ons, so a second dentist is a line item.
2. **Electronic claims start at Black Belt**, and the entry is "Electronic Claims 300 per month per provider". Electronic prescriptions, verifications and patient forms arrive at the same tier.
3. **Appointment reminders are tiered twice.** One-way text reminders come with Black Belt. Two-way texting, recall reminders, missed-appointment reminders, email campaigns and Google reviews are Patient Manager Essentials, which is Dojo.
4. **Cloud imaging is Dojo**, at the top of the three.
5. **Data conversion is an add-on.** Both "Practice Management Data Conversion" and "Imaging Data Conversion" are listed there, not in any package.

### What Sensei Cloud costs

> **The page is called pricing and carries no price.** `/packages-pricing/` publishes three package tiers, a full feature list for each of four specialties and eleven add-ons, and not one figure in dollars. Every button on it says "Book a Demo" (consulted 8 September 2026).

That sentence is worth more than a number from a blog would be. Nearly the whole market works this way, and a comparison that quoted a made-up figure here would be wrong within a quarter.

What you cannot work out from their pages, therefore: what the base costs, what a second provider costs, what a claim beyond the 300 costs, and what the gap between Yellow Belt and Dojo is worth.

### What the contract says

Carestream Dental publishes its Master Terms and Conditions in full, which most vendors do not, and it is the most useful document on the site. Four clauses decide things that a demo will not.

> **You get sixty days.** "If CSD holds any of your data, you will have the right to download or otherwise retrieve your data for sixty (60) days after termination. CSD may charge a fee for transferring this data. Thereafter, CSD may delete or otherwise dispose of your data at its sole discretion without consequence" (consulted 8 September 2026).

- **The minimum term is twelve months, and it renews itself.** A Plan "may not be terminated (other than for cause) during the initial 12 months", after which it "will automatically renew for an additional 12-month period", and you leave by "providing us with 60 days' written notice of termination prior to the end of your current term".
- **Non-payment can cost you the data.** Thirty days past due, they may suspend or terminate, and "Such suspension or termination may result in lost Customer data; CSD shall have no liability with regard to such lost data".
- **Features can go away without notice.** "CSD may add or discontinue features, functionality, operations, modules or entire packages of CSD Software without notice."
- **De-identified practice data may be sold on.** The customer "consents and authorizes CSD, at no charge to CSD, to use, access, and provide Customer's practice management data, record data, and other data, to third parties for demographic, commercial, or other use", in aggregated or de-identified form, and "the purposes for disclosure of data subject to this section may include sales and marketing efforts on behalf of CSD".

None of that is unusual for cloud software, and stating it is not an accusation. It is the part of the deal that only exists in writing, and they wrote it down where you can read it.

Two more published details worth having. Training and conversion services expire nine months after the effective date with "No credits, repayments, or extensions". And the packages page says "Unlimited users" while the Master Terms tell you to call sales "To increase your Registered Customer User Limit", which are two statements on the same site that do not obviously reconcile.

## What Dentalpin is

Odontogram, periodontogram, scheduling, clinical records, treatment plans, quotes with signature capture, invoicing, payments, recalls and reports. Plus an AI assistant that carries out tasks against your real data, bounded by each user's permissions.

It runs in any modern browser, on Windows, macOS or Linux. What it does not have is imaging, ePrescribe or anything for US insurance, and it is from 2026 with none of the accumulated edge cases three decades of practice management software buys you.

## Choose Sensei Cloud if

This section is meant seriously, not as a formality.

- **You bill US dental insurance.** eClaims and eVerifications are in the product, and their imaging page promises "quick access to necessary x-rays and photos for claims requiring documentation". We have nothing here, and open source does not fix it.
- **You are already on WinOMS, SoftDent, OrthoTrac or PracticeWorks.** It is the same publisher, the conversion is a service they sell, and Sensei Cloud Apps is explicitly designed to sit on top of those systems with "No migration, no upgrades, no switching required".
- **You want imaging from the same vendor.** X-rays, intraoral photos and 3D images stored directly in the patient chart, viewable next to notes and periodontal charts.
- **You do not want a server in the building.** Their own FAQ makes that the whole pitch, and it is a fair one. Ours needs a machine and somebody who looks after it.
- **You want a phone number with hours on it.** Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 17:00 Eastern, plus email, in-app chat and a 24/7 resource library. That is published, specific, and more than most vendors commit to in writing.
- **Somebody needs to be trained.** Recorded and live training plus setup, configuration and implementation are inside the base package rather than quoted separately.

If any of those describe your practice, stop reading comparisons and book their demo.

## What migrating looks like

Carestream Dental sells the conversion as an add-on and runs it for you, with billing that starts thirty, sixty or ninety days after the effective date depending on the type of order. Ours is a tool you drive yourself, and the difference is real work that lands on somebody.

The `migration_import` module imports through [dental-bridge](https://github.com/dentaltix/dental-bridge), and it is deliberately not one button:

1. **Upload the file** and the system validates it before touching anything.
2. **See a preview** with entity counts and sample rows. Nothing has been written yet.
3. **Review the proposals**: the system maps the source treatment catalogue onto yours and you decide row by row (accept, relink, create new, or ignore). Anything scoring above 0.9 can be accepted in bulk.
4. **Execute**, and the import runs honouring your decisions.

> Step 3 is where most migrations go wrong. Two practices never code treatments the same way, and **a silently guessed equivalence produces invoices that are wrong in ways nobody notices for months**.

If you would rather hand the whole migration to a project team, that is a genuine reason to pick them. Note the nine-month expiry on conversion services if you buy them and then delay.

## The honest part

For a US practice billing insurance, Sensei Cloud is the better fit today and this comparison is not going to pretend otherwise. Claims, eligibility, ePrescribing, imaging in the chart and a support line with published hours are exactly what that buyer needs, and we have none of it.

The picture changes outside that. If insurance billing is not your daily grind, most of what you are paying for is infrastructure you will not use, and the contract you sign for it says your data is yours for sixty days after you leave.

Dentalpin is the other bet: that the software holding clinical records should be readable and ownable by the practice it holds them for. It is younger and it shows. You can [try the demo](https://demo.dentalpin.com) without installing anything, or [stand it up on your own server in three minutes](/en/blog/install-dentalpin-in-three-minutes/) and judge it yourself.
