Dentalpin's odontogram uses **FDI notation** (11–48) and supports permanent and primary dentition. Optimised for four-handed dentistry: everything fits above the fold on displays ≥ 1280 px.

## Treatments

Treatments have fixed clinical colours (filling, endodontics, extraction, crown). Those colours are immutable — changing their meaning would break history and training. If you need more contrast, adjust the icon, not the hue.

## Per-tooth history

Clicking a tooth opens the side panel with:

- Current status (healthy, filled, crowned…).
- Treatment timeline (professional, date, notes).
- Attached images (X-ray, intraoral photos).
