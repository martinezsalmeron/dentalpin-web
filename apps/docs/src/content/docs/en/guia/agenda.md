Dentalpin's calendar is designed for long shifts. That's why compact density is forced and colours are soft: the professional is identified by the vertical rail, not a saturated fill.

## Weekly vs daily view

- **Weekly**: planning. Shows every chair in the clinic.
- **Daily**: chair-side use. Denser, clearer status.

## Conflicts

When two appointments overlap in the same chair or a professional has concurrent bookings, the affected cell gets a 1px `--color-danger-accent` border and a warning icon in the top right. The fill stays at the softened professional colour: identity + alert.

## Reminders

Automatically sent 24h and 2h before each appointment, by email and (optional) WhatsApp Business. Configurable per clinic.
