title: Installation
description: Create your first clinic in Dentalpin and set up the essentials before seeing patients.
---

## 1. Create your account

Go to [app.dentalpin.com](https://app.dentalpin.com) and sign up with your work email. You'll get a confirmation email in under a minute.

## 2. Configure the clinic

Name, tax ID, address, default hours and timezone. You can edit all of this later, but filling it now saves time downstream.

## 3. Invite the team

Under **Settings → Team** add clinicians and staff by email. Assign role (`admin`, `clinician`, `front desk`) and their colour — used by calendar and reports.

## 4. Import patients

Coming from another system? Use **Settings → Import** with a CSV. Format is documented in the [import reference](/en/referencia/importar).
